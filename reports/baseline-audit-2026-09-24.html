Does the Prospective Member Gallons PDF still match the network?

A field-by-field audit of what Interstate actually stores against the 8 baseline profiles in the Roady’s Prospective Member Gallons Calculator, joined to 12 months of real contributed-gallons data, to see which PDF figures hold up and which don’t.

405 active locations audited 69 classified into one of the 8 profiles 67 with contributed-gallons data Gallons source: gallon-report-all-12mo-20260924.csv (2025-09 – 2026-08)
Bottom line: only 2 of the 8 profiles have enough real locations (n≥5) to say anything statistically — and one of those two, Profile 3, is a real candidate for adjustment: the PDF’s 5,000 gal/mo sits above the entire P25–P75 band of actual locations. Profile 4 also falls outside its band but its sample (n=7) is thin enough to treat as a lean, not a verdict. The other six profiles are too data-poor to touch — two (Fuel stop, Large·Highway) have zero classifiable locations in the network at all.

Net: of 405 active locations, 69 have all three inputs populated well enough to land in one of the 8 profiles — a 17% classification rate. The size proxy is the biggest single bottleneck.

Gap in the given rule set: bare state-route numbers (“ND23 / ND8”) and standalone exit numbers with no interstate/highway marker (“Exit 350”) don’t match any of the 6 keyword patterns as specified, so they fall through to Backroad by the rule’s own “everything else” clause. That’s defensible as written, but worth knowing it’s a fallback, not a confirmed classification — only 4 of 232 locations land here, so the effect on the profile table above is negligible.

Methodology notes

  • Location code mapping. The CSV’s “Location Id” column (e.g. R03596) is GallonReportService::getAllLocationsGallonAggregate()’s own format — strtoupper(locationgroup) . str_pad(locationid, 5, '0'). Parsed back to the numeric locationid by stripping the leading letters.
  • Size proxy thresholds. Small/Medium/Large split at the tertiles of Parking Spaces among active Truck Stop (R/S) locations with a numeric value (n=163): ≤30 Small, 31–70 Medium, >70 Large. This is a modeling choice, not a stored classification — treat every Small/Medium/Large label in this report as an inferred proxy.
  • Lane tiers. Diesel Pumps amenity value: 1–2 → “1–2 lanes,” 3–5 → “3–5 lanes,” 6+ → “6+ lanes,” matching the PDF’s own tier boundaries exactly.
  • Gallons averaging. Every location in the CSV has all 12 months present (no partial-year gaps) — some months are genuine $0 (churn, onboarding gaps within the window), and those zeros are included in the average as instructed, which is why several profiles show P25 near $0 despite a healthy median.
  • Region mapping. Supplied directly by the requester, not derived — Northwest (WA/OR/ID/MT/WY/AK), West (CA/NV/UT/CO/HI), Southwest (AZ/NM/OK), Texas (TX), Upper Midwest (ND/SD/MN/WI/MI/IA), Midwest (NE/KS/MO/IL/IN/OH), Northeast (ME/NH/VT/MA/RI/CT/NY/NJ/PA/DE/MD), Southeast (VA/WV/KY/TN/NC/SC/GA/FL/AL/MS/LA/AR). 2 active locations have locationstate = '-1' (placeholder) and were excluded from any regional grouping.
  • busDevGallonsConfig.js / BDPG_BASELINE_TABLE live in the roadys-command-center repo, not interstate — confirmed absent here before writing this report rather than assumed. This audit is data-only; applying any baseline change is Command Center’s side of the work.
